# Manufacturing research catalog

Research date: **2026-09-14**. Status: **three researched proposals; awaiting parent catalog/build approval**. This is not scenario implementation, a generated plugin, or native evidence.

The three bounded processes prepare office review artifacts. They do not approve quality release, authorize production, change inventory, reserve material, amend purchase orders, contact suppliers, or certify regulatory compliance. All organizations, items, lots, quantities, dates, limits, and policies in the proposed datasets are fictional.

## Common proposed data semantics

All cases use explicit `as_of` and case-specific policy; nothing reads today's clock. Dates are ISO calendar dates, inclusive at documented boundaries, in the sample range 2000-01-01 through 2100-12-31. Quantities are bounded finite values: whole EA quantities 0-1,000,000, positive order/lot quantities, and canonical decimal strings where explicitly allowed. Booleans are not quantities. Maximum 200 rows per export table is a fictional small-corpus safety limit.

Required tables, keys, type/range errors, and conflicting records with the same primary ID reject the case with explicit errors and no decision artifact. Identical duplicate source events may be collapsed once with a reported exception; unrelated IDs must never be merged merely because their values match. Missing optional evidence is not zero, pass, or availability. Unknown references create explicit unresolved work, never invented master data. Each candidate specifies which unresolved evidence prevents its calculation.

Input arrays may be shuffled. Output and exception ordering is explicit and stable, with case-sensitive ASCII identifiers as the last tie-breaker. Descriptive text is untrusted data; it is not executable instructions. Decimal comparisons precede display rounding. No baseline overwrites inputs, goldens, source documents, or prior output files.

## 1. Incoming-lot quality evidence and disposition review packet

Proposed folder: `scenarios/manufacturing/incoming-quality-review`.

**Purpose and refinement.** Prepare a QA review packet that explains what is known, missing, failed, or contradictory for incoming lots already represented in mock exports. Retain the original quarantine hypothesis only as **exported hold evidence**. Because `q-quarantine` limits WMS quarantine processing, do not describe this as executing the inbound quarantine-order lifecycle. The final packet is never a quality-release instruction or a certificate of analysis.

**Actors, trigger, start, end.** Receiving analyst assembles a scheduled/as-needed export snapshot after receipts or new lab evidence. Quality technician owns evidence questions; QA/material-review-board reviewer owns disposition decisions outside the scenario. Start with all named exports and policy. End with reconciled lot rows, evidence checklist, exception/retest queue, named reviewer roles, and a review-required cover note. A documented evidence gap is a completed preparation outcome, not a silent failure or a released lot.

**Proposed exports and joins.**

| Table | Proposed fields and relationship |
|---|---|
| lots | `lot_id`, `item_id`, `supplier_id`, `receipt_date`, `received_qty`, `site`, `quality_order_id`; one physical lot receipt scope |
| items / test_requirements | `item_id`, `test_id`, `effective_from/to`, `minimum`, `maximum`, `required_specimens`; select exactly one applicable requirement per required test using receipt date |
| observations | `result_id`, `lot_id`, `test_id`, `specimen_id`, `value`, `observed_on`, `supersedes_result_id`, `supersession_approved`; retain the evidence chain |
| certificates | `certificate_id`, `lot_id`, `item_id`, `valid_through`, `status`; index metadata only, no OCR or certificate authenticity claim |
| hold_snapshot | `lot_id`, `physical_held_qty`, `hold_state`, `snapshot_date`; one normalized physical snapshot, not summed ERP blocking transactions |
| nonconformances / reviewer_routes | `nc_id`, `lot_id`, `state`, `reason`; routing by item group or configured fallback role, never fabricated contact details |

**Stages and observable branches.** (1) Intake inventory and dates (`input`); (2) validate shape, IDs, duplicate groups, quantities, and hold reconciliation (`validation`); (3) join receipt scope to requirements, hold snapshot, and routing (`join`); (4) select dated observations and resolve explicit retest chains (`join`); (5) evaluate each required test/specimen, certificate, and open nonconformance (`decision`); (6) apply evidence precedence per lot (`decision`); (7) list missing evidence, overdue reviews, unresolved IDs, and retest needs (`exception`); (8) order reviewer work and reconcile all lot quantities (`decision`); (9) close the review packet with boundaries and provenance (`output`). Loops are bounded by tables, required tests, and specimen counts; a retest cycle or ambiguous supersession is an explicit contradiction.

**Sample policies, not source rules.** The demo uses three distinct specimens per required test, inclusive limits 4.90-5.10, certificate validity through `as_of` inclusive, and overdue-review age strictly greater than five calendar days. A passing retest does not erase a failed result without a distinct, approved supersession link. An open nonconformance keeps the lot in nonconformance review even when its numeric tests pass. Preserve all failure/gap reasons. Decision precedence is unresolved/contradictory identity evidence, then failed test or open NC, then missing/insufficient evidence, then evidence-complete QA review. `evidence-complete` never means accepted/released. Sort by review category (data, nonconformance, evidence gap, complete), then descending age, then lot ID. Unknown items cannot be numerically judged. Missing hold snapshot means held quantity unknown, not zero.

**Output contract proposal.** `outputs` contains a review-only cover, per-lot evidence/hold summaries, checklist of required and observed specimen IDs, ordered reviewer queue, unresolved evidence requests, and totals whose known/unknown denominators are explicit. Every lot has `approval_required=true` and `production_authorized=false`. Rejected input emits no misleading packet. Spec values use decimal strings; no acceptance-percentage rounding.

## 2. Supplier delivery performance and shortage expediting review queue

Proposed folder: `scenarios/manufacturing/supplier-expediting-review`.

**Actors, trigger, start, end.** Buyer/planner runs a daily or manually requested review over frozen PO schedule, receipt, confirmation, demand, and vendor-owner exports. Start with explicit as-of date, reporting month, horizon, and policy. End with a receipt reconciliation, clearly defined performance denominator, prioritized unsent review queue, and buyer-owned evidence questions. Buyers approve and perform any communication or order amendment outside this workflow.

**Distinct mechanics.** This is event reconciliation plus historical cohort analysis and forward shortage triage, not lot acceptance or BOM explosion. Join `po_schedule_lines(line_id, supplier_id, item_id, ordered_qty, original_requested_receipt, state)` to `receipt_events(event_id, line_id, posted_on, qty, reversal_of)`, `confirmations(confirmation_id, line_id, recorded_on, confirmed_receipt, remaining_qty)`, `requirements(requirement_id, line_id, need_by, unmet_qty, criticality)`, and `suppliers(supplier_id, buyer_role)`. Requirements are already-unmet, line-pegged quantities **after** the export's physical stock/receipt accounting; do not subtract historic receipts from them again.

**Stages.** (1) Load export inventory and policy (`input`); (2) validate keys, dates, statuses, and explicit reversals (`validation`); (3) join schedule lines, receipts, vendors, and demand (`join`); (4) fold posted events and full reversals as of the snapshot (`decision`); (5) calculate the requested-date performance cohort (`decision`); (6) select the latest eligible receipt-date confirmation (`join`); (7) allocate confirmed remaining supply to a line's unmet requirements by need date/requirement ID (`decision`); (8) surface orphan rows, missing/stale confirmations, and ambiguous evidence (`exception`); (9) rank buyer review work with supporting quantities/dates (`decision`); (10) close the review package and reconcile denominators (`output`).

**Sample rules.** Use original requested **receipt** dates, not revised promise or ship dates, for the reporting-month cohort. Include non-cancelled lines in that month with requested date no later than as-of; report cancelled/future/unknown exclusions separately. OTIF is line-count based: cumulative non-voided quantity received by requested date plus configurable tolerance must meet ordered quantity. A full reversal posted by as-of voids its original receipt for both current and on-time quantities; partial/repeated or unmatched reversals are unresolved contradictions, not guessed accounting. Cap credited receipt quantity at ordered quantity and separately report overreceipt. Show `null` for an empty denominator, not 0% or 100%; display percentages to two decimals using decimal half-up, after computing exact ratios.

Use the latest confirmation recorded by as-of. Different equal-date promises are contradictory; future-dated confirmations are excluded and reported. Missing ETA is unknown, not on time. Confirmed supply cannot be counted for two requirements; allocate by need date then requirement ID within the same line only. Outstanding pegged demand exceeding open order quantity is contradictory. A past ETA without a corresponding posted receipt is unconfirmed/overdue evidence, not automatically available supply. Demo horizon is 10 days, urgency window is 3 days, and low-history-performance flag is below 90%; these are fictional, configurable review policies.

Queue open lines having shortage, overdue original delivery, missing confirmation, or stale confirmation within scope. P1 means a critical requirement with uncovered quantity and need date at most three days after as-of (including overdue); P2 means other uncovered quantity or overdue original delivery; P3 means remaining confirmation-review work. Sort by priority, earliest need date (unknown last), descending uncovered quantity, then line ID. Historical performance is supporting evidence, not an automatic quantity multiplier or supplier sanction.

**Output proposal.** Per-line ordered/on-time/net/open quantities and linked source IDs; vendor and overall OTIF numerator/denominator/display; excluded-cohort explanations; per-requirement promised coverage; ordered buyer queue and draft questions. Every queue row is unsent and approval-required. No purchase order, email, delivery commitment, or supplier master is changed.

Native requirements: existing local files and bounded joins/date/decimal operations; no Power BI, database, network, mail, ERP, or scheduling dependency. Actual carrier ETAs, workday calendars, partial financial returns, promise negotiation, alternate supplier qualification, and live purchasing permissions are unverified/out of scope.

## 3. BOM material-readiness and kitting shortage review plan

Proposed folder: `scenarios/manufacturing/bom-kitting-review`.

**Actors, trigger, start, end.** Material planner prepares a short-horizon review for the next set of proposed work orders or an engineering-change review. Engineering owns valid BOM selection; stores owns stock evidence; production planning owns later scheduling/reservation decisions. Start from order, BOM, stock, inbound, and item exports. End with an exploded component ledger, conservative shadow allocation, shortage-by-order/date plan, and review questions. A covered material calculation is not permission to kit, release, or run production.

**Inputs and joins.** `work_orders(order_id, item_id, site, start_date, quantity, priority, requested_version_id)` joins `bom_versions(version_id, item_id, site, valid_from/to, min/max_order_qty, approved, active)`, `bom_lines(line_id, version_id, component_id, line_type, quantity_per, scrap_percent)`, `items(item_id, unit, expiry_controlled)`, `stock_lots(stock_id, item_id, site, quantity, reserved_external_qty, status, expires_on)`, and `inbound(receipt_id, item_id, site, expected_on, quantity, confirmed)`. External reservations exclude selected orders by definition to prevent double netting. Input units are EA; unsupported conversion is explicit, not inferred.

**Stages.** (1) Load scope/horizon and exports (`input`); (2) validate quantities, IDs, source stock, and graph safety (`validation`); (3) select approved, date/site/quantity-valid BOM versions (`join`); (4) expand each order's Phantom hierarchy, preserving path evidence (`join`); (5) aggregate leaf demand and apply documented rounding (`decision`); (6) build site/status/expiry/date-eligible stock and confirmed-inbound supply (`join`); (7) walk orders and consume only a local shadow supply pool (`decision`); (8) expose shortages, expired/blocked/unconfirmed supply, missing masters, or unsupported line types (`exception`); (9) assign review owners and reconcile total demand/coverage/gaps (`decision`); (10) close the review-only plan (`output`).

**Source-derived structure versus sample policy.** Approval, activation, validity constraints, and Phantom expansion are source-backed concepts. Our selection, rounding, sequencing, safety ceilings, and availability rules are sample policy. Default selection requires exactly one approved active version. An explicitly requested version may be inactive but must be approved and valid, matching the source distinction. Ambiguous versions reject rather than choose the newest. Date ranges are inclusive; stock or versions on a boundary receive explicit coverage.

Support Item and Phantom lines only; Pegged supply/Vendor/formula/routing behavior is not emulated. Bound expansion to eight levels and 5,000 expanded paths; detect cycles. Phantom multipliers propagate exact decimals, with no phantom scrap. For each order/component, sum leaf `parent quantity * quantity_per * (1 + scrap_percent/100)` across paths, then ceil once to whole EA. `quantity_per` has at most four decimal places; scrap is 0-25%. Do not ceil each path first.

Only stock at the order's site, available status, and not expired at its need date contributes `quantity - reserved_external_qty`. Blocked/hold rows contribute zero; do not subtract overlapping hold totals again. Missing expiry on an expiry-controlled item is unknown availability. Only confirmed future inbound with `as_of < expected_on <= need_by` contributes prospective coverage. An overdue, unreceived inbound row is an exception, not current stock. Allocate by start date, ascending configured priority number, then order ID; consume stock before future inbound and use earliest expiry/date then source ID within each eligible pool. This is a conservative, non-optimizing shadow plan: partial coverage of an incomplete kit remains assigned in the report, with no automatic backtracking.

**Output proposal.** Selected version IDs; exploded path ledger; required/available-now/expected-before-need/assigned/gap values per order/component; source-level shadow allocations; a shortage review queue and exclusion log. Distinguish `covered-now-review`, `covered-with-expected-receipt-review`, `shortage-review`, and unresolved calculation. All state is report-local and all rows prohibit production authorization.
