# Privacy notice

Publisher: Brian O'Donnell  
Last updated: September 17, 2026

## Scope

This notice covers Cowork Process Creator and the bounded SEC filing-to-Excel
plugin used for its end-to-end evaluation. It describes their data handling,
not the behavior of every plugin someone might subsequently create.

## Information processed

The plugins process the instructions and files you supply for the requested
task. These can include procedures, demonstration videos, images, spreadsheet
templates, filing URLs, and public SEC filings. Generated plugin source,
reports, and workbooks are task outputs.

The SEC workflow uses public filing material and reads external SEC resources
only as needed for the requested workflow. Requests to those resources are
subject to the source service's own policies and normal request logging.
Do not include credentials or unnecessary personal or confidential information
in demonstration inputs.

## Where processing occurs

Plugin execution occurs within your Microsoft Copilot Cowork account using
the capabilities and connections you authorize. Microsoft 365 processing,
storage, diagnostics, retention, and organizational controls are governed by
Microsoft's applicable terms, its [privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement),
and your organization's policies.

The publisher does not operate a separate input-collection backend,
analytics endpoint, or telemetry service for these plugins. They do not
automatically publish your inputs or outputs to GitHub.

Developer utilities run locally on the files explicitly selected by their
operator. The optional recording viewer serves an allowlisted set of public
sample assets on loopback only. Publishing a sample or downloading a Cowork
result is a separate, deliberate operator action.

## Your controls

Choose what to upload, which connections to enable, and who can access a
plugin or result. Use **Only you** when testing a private plugin. Manage
task files, sharing, and deletion through the host's controls. The publisher
cannot delete Microsoft-managed service records on your behalf.

A generated plugin that introduces additional services or different data
flows needs a corresponding review and notice before use; this notice is
not blanket approval for those changes.

## Contact

Contact the publisher through the
[project issue tracker](https://github.com/boddev/cowork-process-creator/issues).
Do not include sensitive files, credentials, or private account identifiers
in a public issue.
